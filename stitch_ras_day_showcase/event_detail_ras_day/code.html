About the event

Join us for an intensive one-day workshop designed to introduce the fundamentals of robotics design and programming. Participants will get hands-on experience building autonomous line-following robots using Arduino microcontrollers and various sensors.

No prior experience is required. Kits will be provided during the session. This is a fantastic opportunity to network with fellow robotics enthusiasts and kickstart your journey into automation.
